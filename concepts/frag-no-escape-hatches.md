# frag-no-escape-hatches

**Belief:** the surface computes what the agent needs; it never fetches
it. Every verb self-describes from its declaration; affordances render
what is callable now; guides deepen post-dispatch. When the surface is
insufficient, dissolve content into the declarations and the computed
views — never add a verb, blob, fallback, or second channel that routes
around the real path.

**Why:** `contract()` was the specimen — a 100-line doctrine manual
exposed as a runtime verb, justified as "progressive disclosure," called
zero times in every receipt. It was an escape hatch: when doctrine seemed
missing, the fix was more fetchable surface instead of computed surface.
Each such hatch teaches the agent — and the next session — that the real
path doesn't have to work. Missing features are recoverable; escape
hatches are load-bearing wrongness.

**The law:** surface additions are Lars's call, always. "The model keeps
getting it wrong" is evidence the shape or the computed view is wrong,
never a reason to add surface of your own volition.
