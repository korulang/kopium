# frag-wire-claims-need-probe-evidence

**Belief:** every claim the agent's wire contract, guides, and seeds make
about Koru semantics must trace to a probe or a receipt — never to prose we
wrote ourselves. The contract is a hypothesis about the language; the gate
and the interpreter are the language.

**Why:** we taught "never append a continuation arrow to say" while unsure
whether say could sit mid-pipe; we taught guide rows that fired only
after the refusal they were meant to prevent; we let `@"path"` ride the
frontend into a guides file. Each was locally plausible, each was an
assumption wearing a rule's clothes. The wire gap-analysis matrix
(`bench/WIRE-GAP-ANALYSIS.md`) exists because the failure is structural:
teaching surfaces accrete faster than they are verified, and every
unverified cell is a place the model can be handed a rule the language
doesn't keep — or denied one it does.

**Open questions:** does `|>` pipe data or sequence for void steps?
Is say legal mid-pipe (`prev |> say |> next`)? Can `expr: Expression`
sanction an emit tor? Which contract "never/always" claims survive a
clean probe batch? The matrix tracks them; this fragment tracks why the
matrix exists.
